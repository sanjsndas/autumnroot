Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2025

Autumn Root Journal is an independent, ad-free gardening publication. We believe that respecting your privacy is part of respecting our readers. This policy explains what data we collect — and, more importantly, what we don’t.

📌 Short version: We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies. The only personal data we ever collect is your email address — and only if you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter.

1. Who we are

Autumn Root Journal is a digital publication and blog focused on soil health, seasonal planting, and slow gardening. Our website address is https://autumnrootjournal.com. You can contact us at privacy@autumnrootjournal.com for any privacy-related questions.

2. What data we collect (and why)

We keep data collection to an absolute minimum:

  • Newsletter subscription: If you choose to sign up for our monthly email (“Letters from the Garden”), we collect your email address. This is used solely to send you the newsletter you requested. We use a privacy-focused email service provider (currently Buttondown) that does not sell or share your data. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.
  • Contact form: If you use our contact form to get in touch, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide. We use this only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add contact form submissions to any mailing list unless you explicitly ask us to.
  • Server logs: Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit — for security, debugging, and performance monitoring. These logs are temporary and are not linked to any personally identifiable information. We do not use them for tracking or profiling.

We do not collect:

  • Names or personal details (unless you give them to us via the contact form).
  • Payment information (we don’t sell anything).
  • Location data, device fingerprints, or behavioural profiles.
  • Data from third-party trackers, social media pixels, or advertising networks.

3. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookie we set is a small, strictly necessary session cookie to remember your search filter preference if you use the search feature on the homepage. This cookie contains no personal data and expires when you close your browser.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party tracking scripts. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

4. How we use your information

Any information you voluntarily provide is used only for the purpose you provided it:

  • Email address: to send you the newsletter you requested.
  • Contact form details: to reply to your message.

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. We may disclose information if required by law, but we will always notify you unless legally prohibited.

5. Data retention

Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form messages are kept for up to 12 months for reference, after which they are deleted. Server logs are kept for a maximum of 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. These typically include the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and the right to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@autumnrootjournal.com.

If you are a newsletter subscriber, you can update your preferences or unsubscribe instantly using the link at the bottom of any email we send.

7. Third-party services

We use a minimal set of trusted service providers to run the journal:

  • Email newsletter: Buttondown (privacy-focused, GDPR-compliant).
  • Web hosting: Our server host may keep standard access logs (see section 2).
  • Fonts: Google Fonts, which may collect anonymised usage data. You can read Google’s privacy policy here.

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own marketing purposes.

8. Children’s privacy

Our content is suitable for all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The “last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy, please reach out:
