Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2025

Autumn Root Journal is built for slow gardening, not for tracking. This page explains how we use cookies (and similar technologies) — and why you won’t find any advertising or analytics cookies here.

🍪 Short version: We do not use advertising, tracking, or third‑party analytics cookies. The only cookie we set is a strictly necessary session cookie to remember your search filter preference. It expires when you close your browser.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device to remember information about your visit. They can be useful for keeping you logged in, saving preferences, or analysing site traffic. But they can also be used to track you across the web — which is something we deliberately avoid.

2. How we use cookies

We keep our cookie usage to an absolute minimum. Here is the complete list:

  • Search filter preference (session cookie): When you use the search feature on our homepage, we set a temporary cookie that remembers your selected category filter (e.g., “Soil & Compost” or “Seasonal Planting”). This cookie contains only a short identifier — no personal data — and it expires when you close your browser.

That’s it. We do not set any other cookies on your device.

3. Cookies we do NOT use

To be completely transparent, here is what you will not find on Autumn Root Journal:

  • Analytics cookies: No Google Analytics, no Matomo, no Plausible, no internal tracking scripts. We don’t measure your behaviour.
  • Advertising cookies: We don’t run ads, so there’s no need for ad‑targeting cookies or pixels.
  • Social media cookies: We don’t embed social media buttons that track you. No Facebook Pixel, no Twitter/X tracking.
  • Third‑party cookies from embedded content: We don’t embed YouTube videos, Spotify players, or other third‑party widgets that would set their own cookies.
  • Preference or personalisation cookies: Beyond the single search filter session cookie, we don’t store any long‑term preferences.

4. Third‑party services and cookies

We use a minimal set of external services to run the journal. Some of them may set cookies or collect data according to their own policies:

  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google’s servers. Google may collect anonymised usage data (such as your IP address and which fonts are requested) to serve the fonts. Google does not set cookies for this service, but you can read Google’s privacy policy for details.
  • Buttondown (newsletter): If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored with Buttondown. Their email forms may set functional cookies when you interact with them, but only after you voluntarily submit your email. You can read Buttondown’s privacy policy for more.

We have no control over third‑party cookies, but we’ve chosen providers that respect privacy and avoid unnecessary tracking.

5. Your cookie choices

Because we only use one strictly necessary session cookie, there is no cookie banner or consent pop‑up. You don’t need to “accept” anything to use our site.

However, you can still control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, and you can set them to notify you when a cookie is set. Here are links to manage cookies in common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If you block all cookies, the search filter preference won’t work, but the rest of the site will function perfectly.

6. Do we track you across other websites?

No. We do not use any cross‑site tracking, fingerprinting, or behavioural advertising. Your visit to Autumn Root Journal stays between us.

7. Changes to this cookies policy

If we ever change our cookie practices (for example, if we add a newsletter analytics tool), we will update this page and clearly explain what changed. The “last updated” date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
