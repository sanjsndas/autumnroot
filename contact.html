About Autumn Root Journal

Independent, reader-supported, and rooted in slow gardening.

Autumn Root Journal began in 2019 as a printed zine for a small circle of allotment gardeners in the Pacific Northwest. Today it’s a digital publication reaching over 40,000 growers each month — but we still write every article by hand, from experience.

We believe that gardening is less about quick fixes and more about observation, patience, and a deep respect for soil life. Our articles avoid hype and focus on methods that work in real gardens, across climates and seasons.

Elena Marsh is the founding editor. She has been growing food for 22 years and holds a certificate in soil science from Oregon State University. She writes most of the articles and edits every contribution.

We are entirely ad-free. Our only revenue comes from voluntary reader donations and the occasional workshop. This keeps our content independent and free from brand influence.
